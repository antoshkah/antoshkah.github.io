    how?

    add stats to your home screen.

    1. Open this page in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up). If you can't see it, tap ⋯ first.
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    4. If there's an Open as Web App switch, leave it on.
    5. Tap Add.
    6. Open stats from the new icon on your home screen.

    why?

    stats keeps everything in localStorage. no data is stored externally, and there's no account.

    localStorage belongs to one webpage: clearing your browser's history or website data deletes it. you can export now and then to keep a backup.

    everything is offline and on-device.

    (live view coming in 2.0)

    The home screen app gets its own storage, separate from Safari. Stats you add in a Safari tab won't be in the app, and the other way round.

    In a normal Safari tab, Safari can wipe a site's storage if you haven't visited it for a while. The home screen app is kept as long as you use it. Removing the app's icon delete its stats, so export first.

    The home screen app shares storage with Chrome for this site, so it's the same stats whether you open the icon or the page in Chrome.

    Clearing Chrome's data for this site (or all of Chrome's data) deletes your stats. When your phone runs low on space, Chrome can clear site storage too: export regularly.
